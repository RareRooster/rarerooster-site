Privacy Policy for Dot Dot Star

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Rare Rooster (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you play our game, Dot Dot Star.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Dot Dot Star does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not maintain user accounts or profiles.

2. In-App Purchases (IAP)

Our game offers optional in-app purchases (such as unlocking the Pro level pack or hint bundles). To process these transactions and manage entitlements across your devices, we use RevenueCat alongside platform store providers (Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and Amazon Appstore). RevenueCat may collect anonymous identifiers and purchase transaction history to validate and restore your purchases. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card information or payment details. For more information, please review the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. Advertisements

Dot Dot Star may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These ad networks may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising IDs), IP addresses, and generalized location data to serve relevant ads. They may also use cookies or similar technologies.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings (e.g., “Limit Ad Tracking” on iOS, “Opt out of Ads Personalization” on Android, or privacy settings on Amazon Fire devices).

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To help us improve Dot Dot Star, we may collect anonymous gameplay data, device model, operating system version, and diagnostic crash reports. This data is used solely to fix bugs, optimize performance, and enhance your gaming experience.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at rareroosterdev@gmail.com.
